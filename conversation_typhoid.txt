Clinic transcript - evening OPD
Patient: Doctor saab, mere ko 8 din se bukhar hai aur pet dard bhi hai. (Doctor, I have had fever for 8 days and abdominal pain too.)
Doctor: Any other complaints? How is your appetite?
Patient: Appetite is very low and I feel very weak.
Doctor: Any bowel problems?
Patient: Some constipation since three days.
Doctor: I will order a blood culture and a Widal test. The fever with abdominal pain, loss of appetite and constipation suggests typhoid fever.
Doctor: The Widal test came back positive. My diagnosis is typhoid fever. I am prescribing azithromycin.
