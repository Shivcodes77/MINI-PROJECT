Consultation transcript - General OPD, 14 March
Patient: Doctor, I have had a fever for four days and now a rash has come up on my face.
Doctor: Tell me more about the fever. How high has it been?
Patient: It was 102 degrees yesterday. I also have a runny nose and my eyes are red and hurt in bright light.
Doctor: Any cough?
Patient: Yes, a dry cough since the fever started.
Doctor: I examined the mouth and noticed small white spots inside the cheeks.
Doctor: Based on the fever, red eyes, runny nose, dry cough and the spreading rash, my diagnosis is measles.
Doctor: I recommend an IgM antibody test for measles to confirm, and prescribe paracetamol and vitamin A.
Patient: Thank you doctor.
